# Validation plan

## Roster audit
- Expected school count is configurable (initial target: 138).
- School IDs are unique.
- Each school belongs to exactly one conference after alignment is approved.
- Conference sizes match the approved configuration.
- No school is silently added, removed, or renamed.
- Every verified school has at least one checked authoritative source.

## Branding audit
- Every school has primary and secondary colors.
- Hex colors are valid.
- Logo source URLs and usage notes are recorded.
- Missing or unverified logo permissions remain flagged.
- Conference logo assets are out of scope.

## Schedule audit
See `docs/scheduling-rules.md` for game-count, rivalry, duplicate, eligibility, and conflict checks.
