# League Rules

**Status:** Working specification. Items marked proposed require final approval.

## League composition
- Target inventory: 138 schools across 12 conferences.
- Planned conference sizes: six conferences of 12 and six conferences of 11.
- Membership remains draft until the roster audit is complete.

## Regular season
- 12 regular-season games per school.
- 8 conference games and 4 nonconference games per school.
- No divisions; each conference uses one standings table.
- Proposed conference championship: top two teams in each conference play for the title.

## Nonconference games
Schools negotiate opponents for their four nonconference slots, subject to eligibility, schedule-conflict checks, and league approval. Home, away, neutral-site, and multi-year agreements may be recorded.

## Protected rivalries
Protected annual rivalries are mandatory scheduling constraints. Each must be classified as:
- conference game, counting toward the eight conference games; or
- nonconference game, counting toward the four nonconference games.

The rivalry registry must identify both schools, annual status, game classification, and venue rotation terms.

## Postseason
A 16-team playoff has been discussed as a proposal: six automatic bids for conference champions and ten at-large selections. This format and its selection criteria are not final.
